## Notes on the Tricky Ones

- **Marketplace Facilitator Tax (-$401.20):** This is sales tax that Amazon collects from customers and remits directly to the state on your behalf — it was never your revenue. Don't record it as income or an expense; treat it as a pass-through/non-taxable line (or a wash entry: it reduces the gross sale but there's an equal offsetting tax remittance). Including it in your P&L would understate revenue without a corresponding real expense.
- **Refund commission (+$13.50):** When a sale is refunded, Amazon also refunds the portion of the referral fee it originally charged on that sale. This isn't new income — it's Amazon giving back part of a fee you already expensed, so it should net against (reduce) your Referral Fee expense account rather than sit in a revenue account.
- **Currency conversion fee (-$18.75):** This is Amazon's FX/currency conversion service charge (common if you sell or get paid in a different currency than your bank account). It belongs with bank/merchant/payment processing fees, not with the core Amazon selling fees (referral, FBA, storage), since it's really a financial/banking cost rather than a marketplace fee.
